Pass-and-play board game

Trail Quest

2–8 players, one phone. Push your luck and race to the flag.

Players
4
How to play

Everyone shares one phone. On your turn you pick one thing:

  • Move 1, 2 or 3. Safe and exact. Use it to land on the tile you actually want.
  • Push your luck. Roll the die again and again, stacking up steps. Roll a 1 and you lose every step you stacked this turn.

Where you land matters:

  • ⚡Boost — jump 3 more tiles forward.
  • 🕳️Pit — fall back 4 tiles.
  • 🛡️Shield — take one. It blocks the next pit or shove.
  • 🌀Warp — swap places with whoever is in the lead.

Land on somebody and you shove them back 3 tiles, unless they spend a shield. Tile effects never chain, so a boost that drops you on a pit leaves you safe.

First player to reach the flag wins. You do not need an exact roll to finish.
