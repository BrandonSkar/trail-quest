Pass-and-play board game

Trail Quest

2–8 players, one phone. Pick a piece, roll, and race to the flag.

Players
4

Tap a piece to change it.

How to play

Everyone shares one phone. Your turn is three taps:

  1. Roll the die and watch your piece hop along the trail.
  2. Draw a card for the space you land on. Some cards tell you what happens. Most give you a choice, and the choice is usually the whole game.
  3. Pass the phone to the next player.

The colour of the space decides which deck you draw from:

  • 🌲Green, Trail — good news, and often a choice about how greedy to be.
  • 🎲Orange, Risk — a gamble. Take the safe option or roll for a bigger prize.
  • ⚠️Red, Trouble — a setback. You usually pick how you suffer.
  • 🎒Purple, Gear — pick one item to carry with you.
  • 💤Blue, Rest — nothing happens. Catch your breath.
  • ⏩Pink, Shortcut — jump 3 spaces ahead, no card.

Energy ⚡ is what cards trade in. Hold enough and you can buy your way out of trouble, or sprint: spend 2 energy to add 3 to your roll. Energy is worth nothing at the finish line, so spend it.

Gear is the part worth thinking about, because you choose the moment. You can carry two pieces at once.

  • 🥾Fast Boots — skip the die and move exactly 6. Useful when a bad roll would ruin you.
  • 🪢Rope — roll and add 3.
  • 💨Gust — send any player you choose back 4, then take your turn.
  • 🍀Lucky Charm — when a Trouble card lands, throw it away instead. Spending it early is easy. Holding it is braver.

Land on another player and you knock them back 2. Only your die roll triggers a space, so a card that moves you never sets off another card.

First player to reach the flag wins. You do not need an exact roll to finish.
