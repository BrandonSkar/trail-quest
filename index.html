Pass-and-play board game

Trail Quest

2–8 players, one phone. Roll, draw a card, race to the flag.

Players
4
How to play

Everyone shares one phone. Your turn is three taps:

  1. Roll the die and move that many spaces.
  2. Draw a card for the space you land on. Some cards tell you what happens. Others give you a choice, and the choice is usually the whole game.
  3. Pass the phone to the next player.

The spaces decide which deck you draw from:

  • 🌲Trail — good news, and often a choice about how greedy to be.
  • 🎲Risk — a gamble. Take the safe option or roll for a bigger prize.
  • ⚠️Trouble — a setback. You usually pick how you suffer.
  • 💤Rest — nothing happens. Catch your breath.
  • ⏩Shortcut — jump 3 spaces ahead, no card.

Energy ⚡ is what cards trade in. Hold enough and you can buy your way out of trouble, or sprint on your turn: spend 2 energy to add 3 to your roll. Energy is worth nothing at the finish line, so spend it.

Gear is the part worth thinking about. Some Trail cards hand you a piece of gear, and you hold it until you decide the moment is right. You can carry two at once.

  • 🥾Fast Boots — skip the die and move exactly 6. Useful when a bad roll would ruin you.
  • 🪢Rope — roll and add 3.
  • 💨Gust — send any player you choose back 4, then take your turn.
  • 🍀Lucky Charm — when a Trouble card lands, throw it away instead. Spending it early is easy. Holding it is braver.

Land on another player and you knock them back 2 spaces. Only your die roll triggers a space, so a card that moves you never sets off another card.

First player to reach the flag wins. You do not need an exact roll to finish.
